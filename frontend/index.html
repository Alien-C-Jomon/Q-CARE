ⓘ
Safety comes first.

Q-Care is experimental and not clinically validated. Its urgency indicator, if available, is not a diagnosis or a guarantee of safety. Do not use a low score to rule out a serious problem. For severe, rapidly worsening or worrying symptoms, seek help from a qualified healthcare professional or local emergency services.
